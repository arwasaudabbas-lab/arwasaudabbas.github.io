Whispering-Gallery Modes in Hexagonal Cavities

A compact template showing how a scientific post can look on this site.

A whispering-gallery mode is an optical resonance in which light is strongly confined near the perimeter of a resonator. In polygonal microcavities, the field pattern can reflect the geometry of the cavity and the paths supported by repeated internal reflection.

What should be checked?

A field image alone is not sufficient to identify a resonant mode confidently. It is useful to correlate the field distribution with the resonance spectrum and the quality factor of the same mode.
