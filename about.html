About Me

Science, ideas, and things worth remembering.

I am a researcher in materials science and engineering with interests in semiconductor lasers, III-nitride materials, photonics, and optical microcavities.

I created this website as a personal scientific notebook: a place to write concise posts, explain concepts in my own words, record books of interest, and preserve ideas that I may want to revisit in the future.

Why I write here

Sharing an interesting book, idea, or scientific thought can start a useful conversation. Publishing my notes also motivates me to revisit topics carefully and communicate them more clearly.

Writing principles

I aim to revise each post at least once, keep posts concise, cite sources and references, and make technical topics interesting and accessible to the reader.
